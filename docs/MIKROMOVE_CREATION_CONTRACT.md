# MIKRO/MOVE · creation contract and SEO brief
Date: 2026-10-29 · Target: independently branded microsite at /mikromove, preserving the existing FitMatch production home.
## Why
Greek consumer problems: small-space exercise setup; sedentary lifestyle and lack of time; noise/damping; convenience; working-day fatigue; environmental sleep conditions. Avoid disease-treatment claims. Greece demand evidence: Eurobarometer Sep 2026 (65% Greece never exercise); MINOBI 2025 ~30% good sleep among surveyed workers; ELSTAT/Eurostat 2025 online shopping adoption. The evidence does NOT establish an unsupplied niche nor product purchase intent.
## Commercial reality
Supabase SocialMarket holds 334 historical merchant commission records, 36 fitness source examples (32 public historical) and legacy inventory outside this niche. Named programs Athlos Sport, Mandellos Sports, SportsPoint, Rundome, Medical.gr etc have **unverified program IDs or approvals**, so there are 0 approved money-earning SKUs and 0 verified current stock. Never falsely claim 100 active products.
## Source-led solution
- 10 common Greek problem pathways, with 10 precision product profiles each = 100 SOURCING TARGETS (not buyable SKUs).
- 100 deterministic advice profiles for test coverage; no mandatory AI model/tool calls.
- Live historical records from the existing public RLS-limited Supabase feed projection; show observed dates, prices and source uncertainty. No merchant payout values or tracking URLs are exposed or invented.
- Product profile scarcity is HYPOTHESIS, not proven. Current Greek comparison marketplaces already list many walking pads.
## Distinctive visual DNA
Brand MIKRO/MOVE; editorial deep charcoal, citrus-green accents, large condensed-looking Greek typography via Manrope, hero CSS sculptural capsule (no asset licensing conflicts), deliberate strong editorial rhythm rather than SaaS gradient cards. Compact app layout; mobile custom composition, keyboard-accessible buttons, reduced-motion support.
## Conversion funnel
Pains -> space and noise constraints -> 3 target ideas -> actual historical source examples if any -> save transparent research shortlist; only verified approved offers later get genuine purchase links. Budget only applies to observed historical prices, not unsourced archetypes.
## SEO scope
One unique interactive tool and one researched actionable decision guide, each with real source references, metadata/keywords, sitemap. No 100 thin pages or fabricated reviews/product schema. Main FitMatch home stays noindex; mikromove has indexable metadata. Canonical guide maps to deployed production origin. No performance/ranking guarantees.
## Validation
CI TS/build/tests; accessibility and mobile review from actual rendered browser to the degree the runtime allows. Confirm production route, fallback when no historical items, 100 targets and 10 groups. Full supplier product discovery, merchant approval and 100 actual SKU validation remain separate gating tasks.
