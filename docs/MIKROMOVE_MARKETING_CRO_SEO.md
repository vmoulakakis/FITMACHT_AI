# MIKRO/MOVE — Premium art direction, CRO, affiliate and SEO playbook

**As of:** 2026-10-29. Audience: Greek market, consumers with small living spaces, desk work and preference for practical movement/wellness.

## Creative thesis

Λίγος χώρος. Πολλή ζωή. MIKRO/MOVE is a problem-to-solution decision experience, not another fitness catalog.

Visual identity: forest ink #1c271f, botanical lime #d6f575, chalk #f7f8f1; editorial Greek-capable Manrope and DM Sans, geometric original animated sculpture, meaningful hierarchy, custom mobile composition, focused transitions and reduced-motion support. No generic SaaS gradients or irrelevant 3D.

## Choice architecture and ethical persuasion

Recognize a real problem first, ask three reversible questions, display three explainable routes rather than fifty random product cards, give a lower-friction free/no-purchase alternative, show why an option fits and what to verify, allow self-directed copy and native share of a consented link with generic room/budget preferences. Do not collect medical data. Never use fake countdown timers, reviews, urgency, scarcity, clinical claims, savings or unverified stock.

## Commercial requirements

User wants at least €10 commission per genuine paid conversion, not merely a retail price. Merchant eligibility must be backed by actual Linkwise program ID, approval, current category-specific terms, basis incl/excl VAT, cap, refund/cancellation rules and attribution window. Use 10€ as hard gate on verified *conservative net payout*. An arithmetic model cannot override missing approval.

Public research laboratory /mikromove/sourcing screens ten historical stored Mybrand.shoes SKUs (source 2026-10-22) and ten newly observed merchant-page products (observed 2026-10-29), all marked pending. Archived 8% merchant terms from 2026-09-17 and hypothetical 24% VAT plus 15% reserve can mathematically pass €10 but cannot prove a real approved payout. No actual buy links or tracking redirects are enabled.

## Actual 100-product strategy

One hundred precision product sourcing profiles in ten Greek pain families, each a documented procurement target rather than a fabricated SKU. Validate actual merchant offers against dimensions, home-space fit, stock, compliance, return policy, after-sales service, price and approved payout. Some premium machines may be commercially attractive yet unsuitable for small flats, and should be excluded from user recommendations.

## Content and organic search

Launch a decision tool and a truly useful original measurement guide first. Index only original pages. Source and date claims. SEO intents: γυμναστική σε μικρό σπίτι; μικρό home gym; αθόρυβη άσκηση σε διαμέρισμα; διάδρομος χωρίς χώρο αποθήκευσης; walking pad θόρυβος; άσκηση στην τηλεργασία; ελαφριά κινητικότητα; κωπηλατικό ή ποδήλατο για μικρό χώρο; χαλάρωση στο σπίτι; εύκολη αποθήκευση οργάνων. Do not expand into thin templated 100 SEO pages before original evidence. Current Skroutz already lists dozens of walking pads, so the differentiator is decision-fit, not absence of products.

Search Console/Google Ads keyword planner are not connected for this site in this task; no invented search volume or ranking. Maintain canonicals, sitemap, share OpenGraph 1200x630 image, accessible readable HTML, useful internal linking and no merchant Product schema before verified data.

## User-controlled sharing and content distribution

Personal profile link: pain, generic room size, sound tolerance and budget; no account and no health diagnosis. Native share or clipboard fallback. Instagram carousel for a buyer checklist; Reel for a real measured small-space use case only if footage licensed; Facebook post for the relatable home problem; Pinterest storage guide; short YouTube demo of independently tested equipment; SEO article with truthful measurements. No bot engagement, spam DMs or fake user testimonials.

## Experiment backlog

A/B headline based on specific problem vs broad wellness; quiz length 2 vs 3 steps; why-this-fits explanation order; shortlist share position; free-option presence; compare product types by fit and space, not biggest affiliate payout. Guardrail: no decline in user decision quality or verified trust. Never run tests without privacy-respecting instrumentation and sample sufficiency.

## KPI definitions and tracking status

Proposed events: mm_view, mm_choose_pain, mm_quiz_advance, mm_explanation, mm_no_buy, mm_share_intent, mm_share_success, mm_shortlist_saved, mm_research_open, mm_verified_outbound, mm_accepted_commission. These are an IMPLEMENTATION PLAN, not live analytics. Outcome metrics: relevant solution rate, quiz completion, share/save rate, verified merchant offer coverage, approved payout per accepted order, net realized affiliate revenue after reversals. Zero verified commission currently.
